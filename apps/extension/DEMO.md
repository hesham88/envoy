# 30-second demo capture

Setup: a Chrome profile with the built-in model available, the extension loaded unpacked from `dist-dev` (developer build, so the cloud step works), and a page with a text box (a test page or ChatGPT).

1. **0–4 s.** Type into the text box: "Hi, I'm Mona Adel (mona.adel@example.com). My rent is EGP 8,500."
2. **4–8 s.** Click the Envoy toolbar icon, switch **Envoy on this site** on, accept Chrome's prompt, reload the tab.
3. **8–14 s.** Focus the text box and click the seal chip (**Envoy off**). The ring locks on in amber; the card lists the name, email and amount with their tags, and shows what the site receives now and after.
4. **14–20 s.** Press **Replace 3 in the field**. Motes fly into the field, the ring turns green, the chip says **Protected**, and the field holds tags only.
5. **20–26 s.** Select a paragraph on any page, right-click, **Ask Envoy about this selection**. The side panel opens with it as the document; ask a question and show the flight recorder and the approval card.
6. **26–30 s.** End frame: "Ask the cloud. Keep your secrets."
